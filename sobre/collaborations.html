Collaborations

EM Research & Tech establishes strategic partnerships with leading universities in Latin America and Europe to foster research, innovation, and technology transfer.

Universidad Nacional de San Antonio Abad del Cusco (UNSAAC)

Strategic collaboration focused on engineering research, scientific training, and the development of applied technological solutions in telecommunications, electronics, and artificial intelligence.

This partnership strengthens regional innovation and promotes the integration of academic research with real-world engineering challenges in Peru.

Universitat Politècnica de València (UPV) – Spain

International collaboration in advanced engineering, signal processing, artificial intelligence, and electromagnetics.

This partnership enables joint research projects, academic exchange, and the development of high-impact scientific publications.

Universidad Politécnica Salesiana (UPS) – Ecuador

Collaboration in applied research, embedded systems, automation, and intelligent engineering solutions.

This partnership supports technological development and innovation projects with strong societal and industrial impact in Latin America.

Global Collaboration Vision

Our collaboration network connects Latin America and Europe through science, engineering, and innovation, building a shared ecosystem for technological progress.
