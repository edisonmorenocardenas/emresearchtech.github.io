Who We Are

EM Research & Tech is a private organization focused on research, technological development and consulting.

Founded in Cusco, Peru (2025), we operate with a flexible and innovation-driven structure, combining scientific expertise and entrepreneurial vision to create impactful solutions in engineering and technology.

Vision

To become a leading reference in Latin America for technological innovation and integration between science and society.

Values
