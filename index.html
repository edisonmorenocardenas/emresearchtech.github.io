EM Research & Tech: Engineering, Research and Technology

Our focus

At EM Research & Tech, we drive technological and scientific innovation through excellence in applied engineering and experimental development.

Our work is built on four key pillars: applied research, technological development, innovation consultancy and knowledge transfer. At the intersection of science and technology, we connect knowledge, industry and society to transform our advances into real-world solutions that impact the way companies, institutions and communities harness technology to improve their performance and quality of life.

Fuzzy Logic Control for Low-Speed Motor Drives

Mamdani fuzzy V/f control with PSO for induction motors. Stable low-speed operation (6.06%) with improved dynamics over PI control.
